JHA Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Purpose and access

JHA is a personal job-search assistant under development for its owner's use. It requests read-only Gmail access to find and analyze job-alert emails and identify relevant opportunities. This permission technically allows reading the mailbox, although the intended use is job-search emails. It does not allow sending, modifying, or deleting emails.

Use of email data

Email content and information extracted from it are used to support the owner's job search. Depending on the final implementation, relevant content may be sent to an AI service such as the OpenAI API for analysis. Service providers and their data-handling settings have not yet been finalized.

Storage and retention

The application is still being developed. Storage locations, retention periods, and deletion procedures have not yet been finalized. This policy will be updated to describe those practices before ongoing automated processing begins.

Control over access

The owner can revoke JHA's access through their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access prevents future access but does not automatically delete information previously stored by the application or its service providers.
